    How to play

    • Guess any of the 6,874 players who've been in a Premier League squad since 1992.
    • Nation gets an amber outline for the right continent.
    • Club is the PL club they spent most seasons at (ties go to the club they made most appearances for). An amber outline means the mystery player also played for your guess's club.
    • Born and First PL season get an amber outline within 2, and PL goals within 5. The word tells you which way to go.
    • Set Era to 2010–present to only get players who played in the Premier League from 2010/11 onwards. Pick a difficulty: Normal (well-known players and regulars with 75+ PL appearances) or Anorak (anyone with 10+ PL appearances). You can play up to 50 players a day.
